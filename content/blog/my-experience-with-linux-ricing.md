I love [Hackers (1995)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackers_(film)). It's a *supremely* cheesy film following the story of a group of high school hackers who get swept up in a plot to steal millions of dollars from a major corporation, and the journey they take to clear their name. My dad showed me the film when I was a kid, during the initial years when I was getting into coding, and it was one of many factors that got me so deeply entrenched in programming. It doesn't really hold up nowadays - the plot is pretty uninteresting, and its depiction of hacking is very extra - but I still love it because of its overly 90s visuals.


![Gibson Server](https://madmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2019/02/Hackers_07.jpg)
<sub>Source: Hackers, Dir. Iain Softley, 1995 © Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer, Inc.</sub>

It's stuffed full of these futuristic and colourful portrayals of cyberspace that made writing code and exploring the internet seem so *fascinating*.
